Frequently asked

Is NXC real money?

No. NXC (Nexaura Coins) is virtual in-game currency used only inside the NEXAURA game economy. It has no cash value, is not a security, and is not a financial product.

What happens when a card completes?

After the 30-day period the card reaches COMPLETED state and the configured completion value in virtual NXC unlocks. You claim it once, it is credited to your virtual wallet, and the card becomes CLAIMED.

How is the daily reward calculated?

Accrual is continuous and decimal-safe: earned = completionValue × elapsed / 30 days. At exactly 30 days the earned amount equals the configured completion value with no rounding drift.

Can I buy a card I can't afford?

No. Cards you cannot afford are shown as unavailable and the purchase action is blocked, with a clear message.

How many referral levels are there?

Exactly one. Only your direct referrals qualify, and only after their first card purchase. Second-level referrals earn nothing.

Where is my game profile stored?

Right now your game profile lives in this browser only. Once a backend is connected (Firebase or Supabase, from the operator panel) all balance-changing operations move to authenticated server logic.

Virtual game economy notice. NEXAURA is an entertainment product. Nexaura Coins (NXC), game cards, progression and completion values are all virtual. Nothing here is an investment, security, ROI, passive income or guaranteed financial return.
